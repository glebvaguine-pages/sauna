        <p>

        </p>

          Layout (top‑down): back wall = 7 ft sauna with two‑tier bench; heater in front‑right interior corner; door front‑left; Coldture tub (63×29.5 in) centred in front; chiller (~24×13 in) to front‑right; 3 ft walking strip in front; side paths inside gazebo. [web:178][web:179]

Project Summary

This project builds a 7×5 ft cedar hot sauna inside a 10×8 ft gazebo, with the heater correctly positioned inside the front‑right corner, plus a Coldture Classic cold plunge bundle (tub + chiller) centred in front of the sauna with safe walking paths around everything. [web:178]

The plan assumes a hardtop 10×8 gazebo is already installed and focuses on the sauna structure, interior finish, electrical, and cold plunge integration while keeping clear 3 ft circulation zones. [web:178][web:184]

Layout (top‑down): back wall = 7 ft sauna with two‑tier bench; heater in front‑right interior corner; door front‑left; Coldture tub (63×29.5 in) centred in front; chiller (~24×13 in) to front‑right; 3 ft walking strip in front; side paths inside gazebo. [web:178][web:179]

Build Checklist

Work phases for a 7×5 sauna under 10×8 gazebo with Coldture plunge.

Phase 1 – Planning & Permits Start here

  • Confirm gazebo is structurally sound and truly 10×8 ft clear span.
  • Verify local permit / electrical requirements for sauna + 240 V heater. [web:16]
  • Select heater model (~9 kW) and confirm clearances for front‑right interior corner. [web:16]
  • Order Coldture Classic bundle; confirm delivery lead time. [web:178]

Phase 2 – Foundation & Layout

  • Mark 7×5 ft sauna footprint centered along back of gazebo.
  • Excavate/level and install 3–4 in compacted gravel pad for sauna platform.
  • Build 5×7 ft PT floor frame, level & anchor as needed.
  • Install 3/4 in exterior plywood subfloor.

Phase 3 – Framing (Walls & Roof)

  • Frame 7×5 ft walls with 2×4 studs, allowing for 26 in door front‑left & window front‑right.
  • Install 2×6 ceiling joists and blocking for benches and heater.
  • Sheath exterior with OSB/plywood if required for composite system.

Phase 4 – Electrical Rough‑In ⚡

  • Electrician pulls 50 A 240 V circuit from main panel to gazebo disconnect. [web:16]
  • Run heater feed & control wiring inside sauna walls to heater location.
  • Install sauna‑rated light & switch box.
  • Plan 15–20 A GFCI for Coldture chiller; rough‑in outlet near front‑right corner. [web:179]

Phase 5 – Insulation & Vapor Barrier

  • Install R‑13 batts in all walls and R‑19 batts in ceiling.
  • Staple foil vapor barrier continuously over warm side of insulation.
  • Foil‑tape all seams and around fixtures; foam any penetrations.

Phase 6 – Ventilation

  • Cut openings for high intake vent and low exhaust vent on back wall. [web:16]
  • Install vent sleeves, grilles and optional inline exhaust fan.

Phase 7 – Cedar Interior

  • Install cedar T&G on ceiling first, perpendicular to joists.
  • Install cedar on walls, leaving gaps for door, window, vents & light.
  • Add cedar trim: corners, base trim, door/window casing, vent covers.

Phase 8 – Benches & Heater Guard

  • Build two‑tier back bench (16 in lower, 20 in upper, 36 in high). [web:80]
  • Sand all seating surfaces & radius exposed edges.
  • Construct cedar heater guard and anchor around front‑right interior heater zone.

Phase 9 – Door, Window & Fixtures

  • Install pre‑hung 26 in glass sauna door at front‑left, verify swing/clearances.
  • Install window unit front‑right, flashed & trimmed.
  • Mount accessories: thermometer, hygrometer, timer, towel hooks.

Phase 10 – Heater & Electrical Final ⚡

  • Set 9 kW heater in front‑right interior corner within manufacturer clearances. [web:16]
  • Load sauna stones per manual and secure guard rail.
  • Electrician completes connections, tests circuits, and passes inspection.

Phase 11 – Exterior Cladding

  • Wrap exterior with housewrap / WRB, tape seams.
  • Install vertical furring strips to create 3/4 in air gap.
  • Attach grey composite panels and trim, ensuring bottom & top ventilation.

Phase 12 – Coldture Plunge Setup

  • Place Classic tub centred in front of sauna with ≈3 ft walking strip and 36 in side clearances. [web:178]
  • Position chiller near front‑right gazebo post; connect hoses & power. [web:179]
  • Fill tub, purge air, and test cooling/heating range (3–42 °C). [web:178][web:179]

Phase 13 – Testing & Tuning

  • Perform first heat‑up to ~180–190 °F, check for leaks, odors, and even heating. [web:76]
  • Verify ventilation by timing how quickly room clears after a session.
  • Confirm Coldture tub can reach and maintain target cold temp.
  • Document photos, as‑built measurements, and keep all manuals together.
